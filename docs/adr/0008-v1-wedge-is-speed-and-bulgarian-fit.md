# v1 competes on speed and Bulgarian-market fit, not features

**Context.** The vision positions the AI suite as the differentiator, but all AI is Phase 2. The v1 feature set (customers, vehicles, repair orders, invoices) is table-stakes that incumbents already offer, raising the question of why a garage would switch to a featureless v1.

**Decision.** v1's competitive wedge is **being dramatically faster and simpler than incumbents, and genuinely localized for the Bulgarian market** (proper фактури, language, and the real small-shop workflow) — not feature count. AI is the retention/expansion hook layered on later, not the acquisition hook.

**Consequences.** This is the tie-breaker for scope arguments: invest disproportionately in the polish and speed of the Repair-Order→Invoice loop and plate/VIN search; resist breadth that dilutes that focus. Features that don't make the core loop faster or more local are deferred. Success metric for v1 is time-to-complete-a-job-and-invoice, not feature parity.
